Two groups are called commensurable if they have isomorphic
subgroups of finite index. Finitely generated commensurable groups are
quasi–isometric, but the converse only holds in some “rigid” cases. I will
present our results and work in progress on the commensurability
classification of right–angled Artin groups and Baumslag–Solitar groups.
Joint work with M. Casals–Ruiz and I. Kazachkov.
