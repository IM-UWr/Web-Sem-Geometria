We consider the binomial model *Γ(n,p)* of a random triangular group,
in which the group is given by a random presentation *⟨S|R⟩* with *n* generators and
relators of length three, such that each relator is present in *R* independently with
probability *p*. We are interested in the asymptotic behavior of the random group
*Γ(n,p)* when *n* goes to infinity and *p=p(n)*. In particular, we show that there
exists a constant *c>0* such that for any *ε>0*, with probability tending to *1*, if
*p<(c−ε)n<sup>−2</sup>* then *Γ(n,p)* is a free group, whereas for *p≥(c+ε)n<sup>−2</sup>* the random
group *Γ(n,p)* is not free.
