We know since work of Wise that *C’(1/6)* small cancellation groups are
cubulable, i.e. they act properly and cocompactly on a CAT(0) cube complex. Certain hyperbolic groups, although not small cancellation groups in the classical sense,
can be seen as *C’(1/6)* small cancellation groups over a free product of cubulable
groups. In this talk, I will present a cubulation theorem for *C’(1/6)* small cancellation groups over a free product of ﬁnitely many cubulable groups. Such groups
act very nicely on a small cancellation polygonal complex with cubulable vertex
stabilisers. I will explain how one can ”combine” the various wallspaces structures
(on vertex stabilisers, on the polygonal complex) into a wallspace structure for the
whole group. This is joint work with M. Steenbock (University of Vienna)
