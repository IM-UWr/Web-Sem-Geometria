This mini-course will introduce the mapping class group of an orientable surface, starting from basic definitions and examples and building up the key toolkit: Dehn twists, generators and relations, and action on curves and arcs. We will discuss the Nielsen–Thurston classification of surface homeomorphisms into periodic, reducible, and pseudo-Anosov types, highlighting the geometric and dynamical features of each.
