Recently, Damian Osajda introduced a combinatorial curvature
condition called m-location (m > 6) for flag simplicial complexes.
According to Osajda's definition, a flag simplicial complex is m-located
if every so called dwheel with the boundary length at most m is
contained in a 1-ball. We shall enlarge this definition of m-location.
Namely, we require that any homotopically trivial loop of length at most
m should be contained in the link of a vertex. Using this more general
definition of m-location, we shall extend some of Osajda's results. We
prove that the universal cover of an 8-located simplicial complex is an
8-located simplicial complex satisfying the SD' property. The SD'
property is a global combinatorial condition on flag simplicial
complexes. Eventually, we show that a simply connected 8-located
complex is hyperbolic.
